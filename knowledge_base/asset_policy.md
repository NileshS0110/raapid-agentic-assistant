IT ASSET MANAGEMENT POLICY

Company-issued laptops, monitors, and other equipment are assigned to employees based on role requirements.

Employees are responsible for safeguarding assigned equipment.

Lost, stolen, or damaged equipment must be reported to IT Operations immediately.

Employees must return all assigned company equipment when leaving the organization.

Employees must not transfer assigned equipment to another employee without IT approval.

Asset assignment information is maintained in the company asset register.
