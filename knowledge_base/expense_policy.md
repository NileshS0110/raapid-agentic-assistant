EMPLOYEE EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY

Employees may claim reimbursement for eligible business-related expenses.

Meal expenses are reimbursable up to INR 250 per employee per day.

Internet expenses for approved remote employees are reimbursable up to INR 1,000 per month.

Travel expenses must be supported by valid receipts.

Claims must be submitted through the finance portal within 30 days of the expense date.

Claims above INR 10,000 require manager approval.

Personal purchases and expenses without valid supporting documents are not reimbursable.
