FINANCE FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: What is the daily meal reimbursement limit?

A: Employees can claim meal expenses up to INR 300 per employee per day.

Q: How long do employees have to submit reimbursement claims?

A: Claims should be submitted within 30 days of the expense date.

Q: Are receipts mandatory?

A: Yes. Valid receipts must be attached to reimbursement claims.
