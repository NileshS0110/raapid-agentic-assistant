ANNUAL LEAVE POLICY — 2024

Employees are entitled to 18 days of annual paid leave per calendar year.

Leave requests must be submitted to the reporting manager at least 3 working days in advance.

Unused annual leave can be carried forward up to a maximum of 5 days.

This policy was superseded effective 2026-01-01.
Do not use this document to answer current employee entitlement questions.
