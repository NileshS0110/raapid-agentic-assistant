EMPLOYEE ONBOARDING GUIDE

New employees must complete the onboarding checklist during their first working week.

The checklist includes:
- Completing personal and employment information
- Reviewing company policies
- Completing mandatory security training
- Setting up corporate email and collaboration tools
- Confirming receipt of assigned equipment

Employees should contact Human Resources for onboarding-related questions.

IT access issues should be reported through the IT support ticketing process.
