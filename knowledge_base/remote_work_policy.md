REMOTE WORK POLICY

Eligible employees may work remotely with manager approval.

Employees working remotely must:
- Remain available during agreed working hours.
- Protect confidential company information.
- Use approved company devices and secure network connections.
- Follow information security requirements.

Employees must obtain manager approval before making changes to their agreed work arrangement.

Approved remote employees may claim internet expenses subject to the Employee Expense Reimbursement Policy.
