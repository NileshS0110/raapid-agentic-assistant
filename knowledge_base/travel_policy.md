BUSINESS TRAVEL POLICY

Business travel must be approved by the employee's reporting manager before booking.

Employees should use approved travel booking channels wherever available.

Reasonable transport and accommodation expenses incurred for approved business travel may be reimbursed.

Employees must retain receipts and submit expense claims within 30 days of the expense date.

Personal travel expenses and unapproved upgrades are not reimbursable.

Travel reimbursement is subject to the Employee Expense Reimbursement Policy.
